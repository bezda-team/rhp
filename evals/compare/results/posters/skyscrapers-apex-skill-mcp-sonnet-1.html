Reading the skyline

01The giant

At 828 m the Burj Khalifa stands 149.1 m above Merdeka 118, and is 57% taller than the tenth-placed CITIC Tower, which reaches 527.7 m.

02The crowd

Below the leader, nine towers are packed into a 151.2 m band, from 678.9 m down to 527.7 m. Ranks 4 and 5 are 1.9 m apart; ranks 8 and 9 tie at exactly 530 m.

03The China run

Five of the ten stand in China. They were completed one a year, from Shanghai Tower in 2015 to Tianjin CTF Finance Centre in 2019.

Nine towers have opened since the Burj Khalifa. None has come within 149 m.
