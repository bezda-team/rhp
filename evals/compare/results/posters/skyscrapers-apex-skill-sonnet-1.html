Worth noticing

  1. Level at 530 m

    Guangzhou CTF Finance Centre and Tianjin CTF Finance Centre are exactly the same height, and CITIC Tower is only 2.3 m behind.

  2. A near tie at No. 4

    Makkah Royal Clock Tower tops Ping An Finance Center by just 1.9 m.

  3. Oldest and tallest

    Burj Khalifa (2010) is the oldest of the ten and still the tallest. The newest, Merdeka 118 (2023), is No. 2.
