Nearly 60% of the day goes to sleeping, leisure and sports combined. Sleep alone takes up 9 hours, the largest share.

03 / Inside leisure time

Half of leisure is TV.

Watching TV takes 2.6 hours a day, about 51% of all leisure and sports time.
