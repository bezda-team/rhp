Mauna Loa, Hawaiʻi · 1960–2024

The air
has changed.

Six decades of rising atmospheric carbon dioxide, measured at Mauna Loa.

2024 annual mean
424.6ppm

34% higher than in 1960.
A growing concentration in the air we share.

The rise since 1960

+107.7 ppm

From 316.9 to 424.6 ppm in 64 years, an increase of approximately 34%.

A threshold crossed

2015 / 400.8 ppm

The first year Mauna Loa’s annual mean passed 400 parts per million.

Before industrialization

≈280 ppm

The pre-industrial reference level. By 2024, the concentration was about 52% higher.

Source: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, Mauna Loa annual mean CO₂, selected years supplied.
ppm = parts per million: the number of CO₂ molecules per million molecules of dry air. Pre-industrial reference is approximate.
