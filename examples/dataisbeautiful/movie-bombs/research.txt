Dates and uncertainty
The graphic reproduces each report's original estimates, not a new forecast as of October.
The lower and upper values are an envelope of explicitly quoted estimates, not statistical confidence bounds.
The Digger and Supergirl sources disagree; the chart does not average their opinions into an invented point estimate.
The Bride! is approximate and is labeled About $90M.
Moana's worldwide gross has exceeded the July report's $250M assumption.
Supergirl's worldwide gross remains below the June report's $200M assumption.
Digger has exceeded the report's $40M worldwide assumption and remains in release.
The source notes retain these assumptions explicitly and do not revise losses through a generic rental percentage.
Theatrical and first-cycle forecasts use different revenue windows and are labeled separately.
