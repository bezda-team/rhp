[OC] The largest comparable loss forecasts I could verify for 2026 movie releases

First top-level comment:

I reviewed loss claims for 30 research candidates and selected the four largest comparable forecasts I could verify in original film-industry trade reporting by 17 October 2026.
All four forecasts include income after cinemas, and they are ordered by the highest quoted estimate.
This is a ranking of dated published forecasts found in that review, not a complete ranking of actual 2026 losses.
Films without comparable verified forecasts remain unknown, rather than being assigned a zero or assumed smaller.

Original reports:
Digger: TheWrap, 7 October, $200M to $215M from one source and $250M from another, including post-theatrical revenue.
https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/digger-tom-cruise-lose-250-million/
Supergirl: Deadline, 29 June, a $125M first-cycle loss forecast including downstream income.
https://deadline.com/2026/06/supergirl-bomb-boxoffice-1236970324/
Moana: Deadline, 12 July, a $100M to $125M first-cycle loss forecast.
https://deadline.com/2026/07/moana-bombs-box-office-1236980125/
The Bride!: Deadline, 8 March, losses could approach $90M after first-cycle home-entertainment income.
https://deadline.com/2026/03/the-bride-box-office-bombs-1236747021/

Solid bars reach the lowest quoted estimate, and stripes extend to the highest.
The empty outline is the common zero to $250M scale, not the film's budget.
The stripes are not statistical confidence intervals.
Supergirl and Moana share second place by upper estimate.
The Bride! is approximate, and Supergirl's point forecast has no artificial range extension.

The reports have different dates and unpublished accounting assumptions.
Moana's July forecast assumed $250M worldwide; its subsequent $323.7M gross is higher.
Digger's report expected no more than $40M worldwide; it has reached $40.8M and is still playing.
Supergirl's Deadline report does not disclose the final gross assumed in its loss model.
I have not recalculated any forecast using those later grosses, or added the forecasts into a total.
Directors and actors are identification credits, not an attribution of blame.

The source audit also covers Desert Warrior, Masters of the Universe, The Mandalorian and Grogu, The Dog Stars and other candidates.
The larger-looking Desert Warrior and Masters claims located used budget/gross gaps or simple theatrical-rental calculations, without a verified comparable first-cycle model.
Their exclusion does not establish that their actual losses are smaller.
Variety's later $100M figure for The Bride! is explicitly theatrical-only, so it is not mixed into Deadline's first-cycle forecast.
https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/digger-tom-cruise-warner-bros-box-office-1236878000/
