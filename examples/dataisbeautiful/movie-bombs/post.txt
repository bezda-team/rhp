Sources: original reporting by TheWrap (Digger, 7 October), Deadline (Moana, 12 July; The Bride!, 8 March), and Variety (Supergirl, 30 June).
These are forecasts published on those dates, not audited final losses or a definitive ranking of the year's biggest bombs.

Moana: https://deadline.com/2026/07/moana-bombs-box-office-1236980125/
Supergirl: https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/supergirl-box-office-bomb-dc-studios-38132/
The Bride!: https://deadline.com/2026/03/the-bride-box-office-bombs-1236747021/

Solid bars reach the lowest quoted estimate; the hatched sections span the estimates quoted in each report.
These spans are not statistical confidence intervals.
TheWrap quotes one Digger source at $250M and another at $200M to $215M.
Variety quotes Supergirl sources at $80M to $85M and $100M to $120M.
Deadline gives Moana a $100M to $125M range and says The Bride! could approach $90M.

The reports use different assumptions and revenue windows.
Supergirl's figure describes theatrical losses, while the other reports include post-theatrical income.
Moana's July model assumed $250M worldwide; its current $323.7M gross is already higher.
Supergirl's report assumed about $200M worldwide, above its current $126.5M.
Digger's report expected no more than $40M worldwide; it has reached $40.8M and is still playing.
I have not recalculated any forecast from these later grosses, or added these unlike estimates into a total.
Directors and actors are identification credits, not an attribution of blame for the financial result.
