Tools: JavaScript for aggregation and checks, rhp (@bezda/rhp) for the HTML chart, modern-screenshot for the PNG, and Playwright for browser and export verification.

The chart includes every complete UTC day from May 1 through October 16, 2026.
Steam Charts samples Valve's concurrent-player API hourly and reduces older chart history to daily and then monthly points.
I used two Internet Archive captures of the same feed to recover the older daily observations.
Explicit source windows exclude the coarse monthly history and incomplete capture days.
Recent hourly observations are grouped by UTC date and reduced to their maximum.
All 46 overlapping dates between source snapshots agree exactly, and the reconstructed monthly maxima reconcile with Steam Charts' published monthly peaks.

These are recorded concurrent-player peaks, not unique players, daily active users, purchases, or all-platform totals.
Each announcement comparison uses the maximum from the adjacent full Saturday-and-Sunday weekends:
May 16 to 17: 13,574; May 23 to 24: 18,389, up 35.47%, shown as 35%.
September 19 to 20: 18,775; September 26 to 27: 37,341, up 98.9%, rounded to 99%.
The displayed percentages are rounded directly from the unrounded calculations.
The June final update was followed by the period's largest recorded peak, 167,867 on June 10.
The full daily series and zero baseline keep the sizes of these rebounds in context.

The October hiring report is marked, but there is no complete follow-up weekend in the data, and the linked official listing currently names Marathon.
It should not be treated as a confirmed new Destiny hiring program or a measured player-count effect.

The September marker uses the official publisher Steam timestamp, September 21 at 16:00:37 UTC.
The Bungie website header says September 17, which is disclosed on the image.
Unvaulting was an announcement of restoration work, not a claim that the vaulted content had already returned.

This shows activity changes around news, not isolated causal effects.
The May statement also previewed the final update, and June brought new content, a complete-content collection, and price reductions.
Discounts, anticipation, promotions, and other changes are not controlled for here.
The adjacent-weekend comparisons also have different numbers of days since each announcement, so the percentages are not a ranking of causal impact.
