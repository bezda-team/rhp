TITLE
[OC] U.S. high-school tobacco use fell 42% from 2022 to 2025, even as nicotine-pouch use rose

BODY
Reported tobacco-product use among U.S. high school students fell from 16.5% in 2022 to 9.5% in 2025.
That is a 42% relative decline, or 7 percentage points.

Nicotine-pouch use rose from 1.4% to 2.3% over the same period, while e-cigarette use fell from 14.1% to 7.1%.
The overall measure already includes both products, so the decline is not simply an omission of pouches from the statistics.

“Current use” means use on at least one of the past 30 days.
These are different samples of students each year, so the chart does not tell us who switched products or how much nicotine they consumed.

Source: FDA's National Youth Tobacco Survey, Table 3 of the 2025 analysis, published June 2026:
https://academic.oup.com/ntr/advance-article/doi/10.1093/ntr/ntag116/8712569

FIRST COMMENT
I made this after seeing people ask whether falling teen vaping rates just reflected a move to pouches.
The total already counts pouches, even those without tobacco leaf.
These are U.S. high school students, not all teenagers or other countries.

I used the latest paper's four-year table and calculated (16.5 - 9.5) / 16.5 = 42.4%.
The three four-year trends are statistically significant; their smaller 2024 to 2025 changes are not.
Categories overlap and should not be added.
