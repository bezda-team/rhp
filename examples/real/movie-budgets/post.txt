REDDIT IMAGE TITLE

[OC] Reported production budgets and worldwide box office for ten big-budget films released in 2026, as of October 17

FIRST TOP-LEVEL COMMENT

Data: The Numbers, captured October 17, 2026.
Annual chart: https://www.the-numbers.com/box-office-records/worldwide/all-movies/cumulative/released-in-2026
Individual movie pages supply the reported production budgets; their worldwide totals reconcile exactly with the annual chart for all ten films in this snapshot.

Selection: I checked the 100 films in that worldwide chart, kept films with a reported budget and a US theatrical release in 2026 by the cutoff, and selected the ten largest budgets.
Sixty screened films had a reported budget, and 59 also met the gross and US release-date criteria.
The result covers budgets from $150 million to $250 million.
Ties are shown in descending worldwide gross.
This is a reproducible sample of reported budgets, not a claim that every film's actual production cost is public.

The Odyssey and the live-action Moana both have a $250 million production budget in this source, while their worldwide grosses were $1,783,979,015 and $323,701,468.
That is a 5.51-fold difference, shown as 5.5 times.
Toy Story 5 is the third film with the same reported $250 million budget, at $1,148,425,809 worldwide.

These are production-budget estimates and cumulative worldwide ticket sales, in nominal US dollars.
Budgets can differ between sources and should not be read as final audited costs.
I use The Numbers consistently rather than mixing different publications' budget estimates.
The source reports Michael at $155 million, for example, while some press reports discuss higher production costs.
Gross includes the cinemas' share, while production budgets do not include marketing.
The ratio is gross divided by production budget, not return on investment or profit.
I have not imposed a generic break-even multiplier or estimated losses.
Some theatrical runs are ongoing, and unreleased late-2026 films are excluded.

Tools: rhp (@bezda/rhp), HTML/CSS and JavaScript; a Node.js collection script; browser canvas to make proportional artwork thumbnails; modern-screenshot for the PNG export.
The film images are promotional poster artwork belonging to the respective studios, obtained from the individual The Numbers pages and reproduced at small size to identify each row.
They have been resized proportionally, with no cropping, stretching, or generated replacement artwork.

Reproducible data, collection code, the individual source URLs, and artwork credits accompany the interactive HTML poster.
