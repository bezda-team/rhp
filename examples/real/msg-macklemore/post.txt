POST 1: msg-headliners.png

TITLE
[OC] MSG said Macklemore is "not an arena level act". 11 of its last 15 music headliners have fewer Spotify listeners than he does

FIRST COMMENT
Madison Square Garden's spokesperson told TMZ on Nov. 6 that "he's not an arena level act".
I took every music headliner in the arena from Jul. 30 to Oct. 31, 2026, and compared their Spotify monthly listeners on Nov. 8 with his 32.3M.
11 of the 15 have fewer, including Mumford & Sons, the Jonas Brothers, Kacey Musgraves and Rush.

What this does not show: monthly listeners are not ticket sales.
Touring bands like Rush and Goose can sell more tickets than their streams suggest, so this tests "not big enough" only on reach.
The numbers are today's listeners, not each act's on the night.
The window is 14 weeks, because that is how far back I could read setlist.fm's listing.
MSG also said its venues have "extremely limited availability", and this does not test that.
Macklemore says the real reason was the shows' purpose; I can't test that either.

Sources: setlist.fm (shows), kworb.net and Spotify artist pages (listeners), TMZ (statement).
Tools: rhp.

POST 2: macklemore-rise.png

TITLE
[OC] Macklemore's Spotify listeners, 2022 to today: a new high of 37.4M in October, and three sold-out European arenas

FIRST COMMENT
Listeners come from kworb's daily table, read from Internet Archive copies, monthly since Sep. 2022 and more often since Aug. 2026.
He has been above 25M since April 2023.
The new high came on Oct. 9, after he was dropped from Ed Sheeran's tour, and it has since eased to 32.3M, about where he was in April 2025.
So this is staying power with a recent spike, not a sudden breakout; he also reached 35.7M in July 2024.

The arenas are Accor Arena (20,300), 3Arena (about 13,000) and OVO Arena Wembley (12,500), against 22,000 at MSG.
Those are venue capacities, not attendance.
The sellouts were announced by Macklemore and carried by the Press Association; I found no box-office report.

Tools: rhp.
