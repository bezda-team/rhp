What is being counted?

This compares the public satellite catalogue in two altitude bands, not the total population of objects in space. It leaves out uncatalogued and unpublished objects, including almost all small debris. It measures the mix of listed objects, not collision risk, orbital density or debris mass.

“Spacecraft” is the catalogue's PAY (payload) category, including working and inactive spacecraft. “Debris” is DEB, its other-debris category. “Other” combines rocket bodies and unknown classifications. No operational-status inference is made from the payload label.

Exact counts and the method

Included: Earth-centred, ORB entries with no decay date and published altitudes satisfying 0 ≤ perigee ≤ apogee < 2,000 km. An object's band uses the midpoint of its apogee and perigee. This is a consistent height summary, not its instantaneous altitude; elliptical orbits can cross band boundaries. Upper band limits are exclusive.

The dot positions are a layout, not physical satellite positions. Each chart has 100 equal dots; each dot represents one percentage point of that chart's total. Split dots show exact fractional shares.
